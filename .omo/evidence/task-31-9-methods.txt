----------------------------------------------------------------
Design contract notes
----------------------------------------------------------------
  - getProgress() touches only std::atomic<int|float> on a single
    alignas(64) cache line (RenderProgress) — lock-free, < 10ms.
  - Both transports share ONE RpcDispatcher instance; the native
    transport guards lazy construction with a std::mutex.
  - JSON-RPC stdio uses LSP-style "Content-Length: N\r\n\r\n<body>" framing,
    with _setmode(_O_BINARY) on stdin/stdout under _WIN32 to keep the byte
    counts intact (no CRLF translation).
  - Preview frame pixels travel via FFI shared memory (T30); the RPC reply
    only carries {ptr, size, width, height} metadata.
